# Respawning an Exhausted Hero

An Exhausted Hero stays out of the match. We do not bring it back into the base after some Rounds.

## Why this is out of scope

- **The Deck is the life of a Hero (ADR 0001).** A Hero does not lose hit points; it loses cards, and every card used is a trade between acting now and surviving later. Exhaustion is the end of that trade. If a Hero came back with a fresh Deck, spending cards would cost nothing in the long run and the central tension of the game would go.
- **A Squad loses when all three Heroes are Exhausted (ADR 0011).** Respawn would remove the match end that this rule gives, or it would need another end rule (a limit on respawns, a timer) that changes the whole game.
- **Winning the focus fight would count for less.** Focusing down one Hero early is a legitimate plan: it pays through the Exhausted Hero's wreck becoming Half Cover and through the Squad playing on with two Heroes. Respawn takes that reward away.
- **The feeling it reports is real but has other answers.** Being out early while the others play is the cost of a match of a few Rounds with an AI today. Shorter Rounds, fewer cards lost to a single focus (Shield, Cover) or something to do for an Exhausted player (watching with the shared view, calling targets) are open for a separate idea.

## What would change this

A real-player multiplayer mode (#311) with long idle times for the player who is out may need a rule for it (a respawn, a spectator role with a job). That would be a new ADR that replaces ADR 0011 for that mode, not a patch on this one.
