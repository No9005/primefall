# 04 — Rules & Avoid-List

## Do

1. Lock one fixed seed for the entire tile set.
2. Keep prompts short.
3. Change only the surface phrase between tiles.
4. Use a thick solid soil base under every tile.
5. Keep the background flat and plain.
6. Generate the grass tile first as the reference.
7. Export every tile as a transparent PNG.
8. Verify every tile against the grass tile.

## Avoid

1. `thin grassy rim` or `green grass along the edges`.
2. `Hay Day mobile farm game art style, cute hand-painted cartoon`.
3. Long, wordy surface descriptions.
4. Letting the seed float or randomize.
5. Asking for tilemap grids, multiple tiles, or seamless repeating patterns.
6. Props, houses, buildings, characters, or flowers on ground tiles.
7. Mixing checkpoints or LoRA strengths mid-set.
8. Upscaling some tiles but not others.

## Mental model

If a tile looks good alone but bad in a map, it is a shape or consistency problem. Return to the fixed seed and matching tile shape.
