# Shield-Based Defense

We use active Shield rather than traditional blocking or hit points. Shield comes from Shield cards (Block was removed, see below); Shield absorbs damage during the current round and fully expires before the next round.

Unblocked damage removes one card from the top of the target’s Deck per point of damage. This keeps defense active and makes positioning, cover, and resource management more important than passive durability.

## Status

accepted. Revised: the Block action (any card as Shield 2) is removed in the card redesign test (#415). Shield comes only from Shield cards, in every mode. The Block mentions in older ADRs are historical.
