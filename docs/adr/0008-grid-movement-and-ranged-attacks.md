# Grid, Movement, and Ranged Attacks

The battlefield is a tile grid (45 by 25 for the Reactor Deck, ADR 0016). Every Hero can use any card as Move 2 instead of playing its primary effect. Allied Heroes do not block Line of Sight or firing lines.

All Heroes use ranged weapons. A normal Attack deals 2 damage to one visible enemy and requires current Line of Sight. Full Cover blocks attacks and sight against attackers at Range 2 or more (ADR 0020); Half Cover reduces incoming damage by 1 but does not block sight.

A tile grid makes range, line of sight, cover, and movement rules explicit and easy to validate.
