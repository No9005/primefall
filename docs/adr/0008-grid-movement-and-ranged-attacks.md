# Grid, Movement, and Ranged Attacks

The battlefield is a 16 by 10 tile grid. Every Hero can use any card as Move 2 instead of playing its primary effect. Allied Heroes do not block Line of Sight or firing lines.

All Heroes use ranged weapons. A normal Attack deals 2 damage to one visible enemy and requires current Line of Sight. Full Cover blocks attacks and sight from the firing direction; Half Cover reduces incoming damage by 1 but does not block sight.
