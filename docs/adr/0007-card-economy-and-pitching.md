# Card Economy and Pitching

Every card has four standard uses: Play its primary effect, Pitch it for 1 Energy, use it as Move 2, or use it as Block 2. A card fulfills only one of these roles during a round.

Pitching always produces exactly 1 Energy. Energy is unlimited in quantity, expires at the end of the round, and is required to play Attack and Utility effects. Hand cards may be retained between rounds, but a Hero draws at most 4 new cards at the start of each round.

When no non-exiled cards remain available, the Discard is shuffled into a new Deck cycle. The next Draw occurs at the start of the following round.
