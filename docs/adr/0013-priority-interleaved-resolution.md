# Priority Interleaved Resolution

Primefall resolves each round through priority-interleaved action queues. During planning, each Hero receives an ordered queue of actions; during Resolution, the engine processes one action from every non-exhausted Hero’s queue per Tick.

For example, if one Hero plans Move, Attack, Block and its opponent plans Attack, Move, Block, Tick 1 resolves the first Hero’s Move and the opponent’s Attack; Tick 2 resolves the first Hero’s Attack and the opponent’s Move; Tick 3 resolves both Blocks.

Actions are validated at execution time. An action that has become illegal—because its target is exhausted, no longer visible, or otherwise unavailable—fails without affecting later actions. Simultaneous attacks both resolve and can both cause damage. Heroes that move do not hinder each other, but a Hero cannot walk through a Hero that stays where it is. A Move (up to 2 steps towards its destination) is taken step by step: all Heroes take step n before any takes step n+1, and positions change only after every Hero of the step has been decided. A Hero may step onto a tile where another Hero stands if that Hero leaves the tile in the same step, so Heroes can follow each other in a corridor, cross, or swap tiles. A Hero that stays counts as standing: a Hero that does not move, whose own step fails, that has finished its Move, that is on a Wait or another action that is not a Move, or that is Exhausted and still on the board. After every step no two Heroes may be on the same tile, so if two Heroes step onto the same free tile in the same step, both fail that step. A Hero whose step fails stops on the tile it stands on for the rest of its Move and counts as standing for the others in this step, which may make the Heroes behind it fail too; this is repeated until nothing changes, so the result does not depend on the order of the Heroes. Walls, Wall corners and Full Cover still block a Move. The rule is decided for all Heroes together, so it is the same for allies and enemies, for the player and the AI, and does not favour a Squad. A `MoveBlocked` event is emitted only when a step fails (by a Wall, a Wall corner or a Hero); it says where the Hero stopped. Knockback keeps its own rule and still stops before another Hero.

A Block takes effect immediately in its Tick and can absorb damage from an attack resolved in that same Tick. This makes action order a core tactical decision: moving into cover before attacking, attacking before an enemy repositions, or blocking before an expected shot all produce different outcomes.
