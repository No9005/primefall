# Hero Exhaustion and Squad Loss

A Hero becomes Exhausted when, at the end of a Resolution, its Deck and Discard are both empty (after the Discard has been shuffled into an empty Deck; ADR 0001) and is out of the match: it plans and acts no more and no longer occupies its tile, so other Heroes may enter and stand on it. Its tile becomes Half Cover (ADR 0020) for as long as the Cover is not destroyed; better Cover on the tile stays, and a Prime Zone tile stays a Prime Zone tile. There is no separate death state and no additional Hand or Deck penalty after Exhaustion.

A Squad loses when all three of its Heroes are Exhausted.

## Status

accepted

This ADR replaces the earlier Hero Death rule and aligns Squad loss with the Deck-as-Life model.
