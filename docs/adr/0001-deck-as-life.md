# Deck as Life

Primefall has no separate hit-point pool. A Hero’s Deck is its life resource: unblocked damage removes cards from the Deck, Overdrive exiles cards, and an empty Deck causes Exhaustion, removing the Hero from the match.

This makes every card use a survival tradeoff and reinforces Primefall’s tactical, high-commitment identity. A team loses when all three of its Heroes are exhausted.
