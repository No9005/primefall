# Deck as Life

Primefall has no separate hit-point pool. A Hero’s Deck is its life resource: unblocked damage removes cards from the Deck, Overdrive exiles cards, and a Hero with no card left in its Deck and Discard is Exhausted, removing it from the match.

Burn and shuffle: Deck Damage moves the top cards of the Deck into the Exile. Whenever the Deck is empty and a card must be taken from it (Deck Damage, Draw), or when a Resolution ends with an empty Deck, the Discard is shuffled into the Deck with the match's seeded random source; the Exile never returns. Exhaustion is decided after that, at the end of a Resolution, so a Hero is Exhausted only when Deck and Discard are both empty (cards in the Hand do not count).

This makes every card use a survival tradeoff and reinforces Primefall’s tactical, high-commitment identity. A team loses when all three of its Heroes are exhausted.
