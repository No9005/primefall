# Hero Roles, Ranges, and Decks

Each Hero has a distinct role and a fixed 12-card Deck with no Flex cards.

- **Bruiser / Vanguard:** Range 3; 6 Attack / 6 Utility; Rush, close-range pressure, Shield, and Zone Control.
- **Controller / Tactician:** Range 2; 4 Attack / 8 Utility; allied Shield, Vision, Cover deployment, Slow, and Marked effects.
- **Assassin / Striker:** Range 5; 8 Attack / 4 Utility; Hidden, scouting, long-range precision, and burst damage. It has two Long Shot cards with Range 7.

Ghost Shot is an Assassin Attack that targets a Ghost’s last known position.

Distinct ranges and deck weights give each role a clear job without making any single Hero universally dominant.
