# Hero Roles, Ranges, and Decks

Each Hero has a distinct role and a fixed 12-card Deck with no Flex cards.

- **Bruiser / Vanguard:** Range 3; 6 Attack / 6 Utility; Rush, close-range pressure, Shield, and Zone Control. Its two Attack Overdrives are one Zone Control effect and one Damage effect.
- **Controller / Tactician:** Range 2; 4 Attack / 8 Utility; allied Shield, Vision, Cover deployment, Slow, and Marked effects. Its Overdrive distribution is 1 Attack and 2 Utility.
- **Assassin / Striker:** Range 5; 8 Attack / 4 Utility; Hidden, scouting, long-range precision, and burst damage. It has two Damage Overdrive Attack cards and two Long Shot cards with Range 7.

The Assassin’s Long Shot Overdrive grants bonus damage when the target is more than 5 tiles away, at Range 6 or 7. Ghost Shot is the Assassin’s third Attack Overdrive and may target a Ghost’s last known position.
