# Cover, Hidden, and Line of Sight

The battlefield uses full and half Cover. Full Cover blocks attacks and line of sight from the firing direction; Half Cover reduces incoming damage by 1 but does not block sight. The Bruiser can permanently destroy Cover through a Zone Overdrive.

Only the Assassin receives Hidden, and only through Utility cards. Hidden lasts up to two rounds unless the Hero attacks or is revealed. This makes the Assassin the team’s deliberate scout and precision threat without turning it into a passive camper.
