# Intro film: storyboard and voice-over (draft 1)

**Status: working draft from a chat with the product owner. Nothing here is decided or built.** The tone follows `README.md` in this folder: grim and quiet in the pictures, satirical in the voice. Names of the companies are placeholders.

## What the film is

- Plays **before the menu** and leads into it. **30 to 45 seconds** (target; it may come out shorter), six beats, one narrator (a woman; dry, warm and a little too calm, like a corporate announcement).
- Tells the world in this order: the Primes fell from the sky (the Artifacts and the alien race behind them), anyone who touches one gains great power, three companies took them over, they rule by paperwork, mercenaries sign on with them for money, and that is why the game is called Primefall.
- **Decided world fact:** "**Prime**" names both the Artifacts and the alien race behind them; the film does not settle which is which, and what happened to the Primes is unknown. "They fell from the sky without a word. We call them Primes."
- The three Heroes are **not named**. They appear from behind, as silhouettes.
- A **second, short film (about 10 seconds)** is a separate task: the ship in orbit between the Hero Select and the map. Not part of this storyboard.
- Look: **everything in 8 to 16-bit style**, in the pixel style of our Heroes (`docs/design/pixel-heroes/`), a fixed frame, slow camera moves. Beats 1, 2, 5 and 6 are quiet and dark (blue-black, red lamps, hazard orange, a cold white for the Primes); beat 3 and the satire of beat 4 are loud and colourful.

## Voice-over in one piece (about 95 words)

They fell from the sky without a word. We call them Primes.

Nobody knows what they were. Only that anyone who touches one can do what should not be possible.

Within a year, three companies owned the sky. Within two, they owned the paperwork.

They never conquered anyone. They issued forms. A permit to stand here. A licence to look there. Oppression, in triplicate.

And when the paperwork fails, there are mercenaries. Three of them. They sign on with the corporations for one reason: to make a living. Money.

Every Prime is a contract.

## Notes and open points

- **Why "Primefall":** the Artifacts of the Primes fell; the logo is **PRIMEF + crystal as the A + LL**, over the curve of the planet; the clouds are our Fog of War with oceans and continents faintly beneath (concept: `images/intro-logo-concept.png`); the claim line is "Every Prime is a contract". The title is the last picture.
- **Mystery stays in the pictures:** the Primes are never explained, only shown (beat 2). The satire sits in the voice and in beat 4.
- **Reduced motion:** the film needs a skip button; with reduced motion it should be skippable from the first frame.
- **Pipeline (nothing is generated yet):** storyboard and voice-over agreed, then the voice (ElevenLabs, only with the owner's explicit go and a cost estimate), then I render the frames in the pixel style, then the music under the finished film (ElevenLabs, again only with the owner's go).
- **Open:** exact length, the company names, whether the officer keeps medals, whether beat 4 may show a crowd (many small figures cost frames).
