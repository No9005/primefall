Swamp: a first probe and four layouts

A dirty swamp with small pools, mammoth trees, hanging lianas, fireflies and small animals that hide in the grass. Drawn in the same 32 × 16 isometric pixel scale as the Reactor Deck. Four layouts on the 45 × 25 board, the last one with high ground.

Probe

Jungle diorama

A test of the look before any layout: one mammoth tree with roots and a canopy, a pool with reeds, a mossy ruin with an artefact, fireflies and frogs that pop out of the grass and hide again.

Layout A

Hollow of the Mammoths

The Crossroads structure in a swamp: four mammoth trees stand where the ruins stood, pools break up the open ground, reeds line the pools. The Prime Zone lies open in a stone clearing, the Outposts on dry stone islands.

Reading

Trunks block Line of Sight and movement like Walls. Root walls are low and make Full Cover. Reeds are Half Cover. The trunks give long diagonal shadows of sight, as the ruins do on Crossroads.

Pools

Shallow water you can walk through. For now only a look. A rule such as Slow or Hidden is open (see the end).

Canopy

Canopy and lianas are drawn over the tiles behind a tree. In the game they fade where a Hero stands underneath.

Layout B

Black Channel

Two dark channels run north to south and cut the board in three. Three fords with stepping stones cross each one. The Prime Zone is on the central island, the Drop Points on the banks.

Reading

Water that blocks, with fords as choke points: fights happen at the stones. A Squad has to decide which ford to cross, and a split Squad is easy to see.

Risk

Water that blocks movement is a new rule (a tile kind). Without it, the channels are only a look and the fords do nothing.

Layout C

Root Maze

Long walls of roots divide the board into lanes. Trees stand where the lanes meet, pools lie in the lanes. There are gaps for flanking and few long lines of sight.

Reading

The closest to a skirmish table with dense terrain: short sight lines, ambushes at the gaps, the Scout Drone has the most to do.

Risk

Playtesters found the old map too crowded with cover. This is dense again, on purpose; it is the layout to try if lane play is wanted.

Layout D · high ground

Canopy Village

Layout A with wooden platforms in the trees around the Prime Zone, joined by rope bridges. A Hero climbs by ladder or by woven lift basket, like the Ewok villages.

Platform Ladder Lift basket Rope bridge
High ground is not in the rules.

The game has one level, and ADR 0020 sets the grid and cover geometry for it. Platforms would need a decision first. A small proposal to discuss, nothing built:

A platform tile can be entered only from a ladder or basket tile (one move, ends the step). A Hero on a platform sees 2 tiles further and sees over Half Cover; an attack from a platform ignores Half Cover of the target. A Hero standing on a platform is visible from the ground at full sight range, so the advantage costs the Hidden state. Baskets are slow (one use per Resolution).

Open

What this does not decide

Water

Look only, a Slow or Hidden effect, or blocking? Layout B depends on it.

Reeds

Half Cover for Heroes, or Hidden like tall grass? Hidden would be a rule of its own.

Canopy

How it fades, and whether it hides the Heroes under it from the player (Fog of War already does that).

Fit

The swamp has no connection to the story of the stricken hauler yet; see the world notes in the repository, docs/design/world.
