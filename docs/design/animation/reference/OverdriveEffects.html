Overdrive
Overdrive is a stronger mode of a card that exiles it (ADR 0003). Gold marks it everywhere: the card, the Hero, the effect. Each Hero has three Overdrive effects; here they are next to what the normal version shows.
The card, armed for Overdrive
In the Hand a card that can be used in Overdrive gets a gold rim that pulses, a gold OVERDRIVE strip at the bottom and shows the Overdrive text instead of the normal one. It says plainly: this card will be exiled.
