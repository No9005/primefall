# Asset Contract

This contract lets the prototype be built with placeholder assets while the final art is produced in parallel. A final asset replaces a placeholder by using the **same file name, canvas size and pivot**. Nothing in the game code may depend on whether an asset is a placeholder.

Placeholders live in `apps/web/public/assets/` and are generated by `docs/design/tools/generate_placeholders.py` (run `python3 docs/design/tools/generate_placeholders.py` from the repository root; it needs Pillow).

## Conventions

- Format: transparent PNG, 32-bit alpha, no baked background.
- Projection: 2:1 isometric. One tile diamond is **128 x 64 px**. One consistent pixels-per-unit scale for everything.
- Light comes from the top left; contact shadows are subtle and part of the sprite.
- The pivot is given as a Pixi anchor `(x, y)` in the manifest. Hero anchor `(0.5, 1)` is the bottom vertex of the footprint diamond of the Hero's tile.
- Heroes face south-east. Other facings are produced by mirroring (`scale.x = -1`); directional variants are out of scope for the MVP.
- Squad colour is not baked into sprites. The renderer draws a ring under each Hero (own and allied Squad teal, enemy Squad red).
- Hidden, Marked, Slow and Shield are separate VFX sprites or filters, not baked pose variants.
- Animation frames (later) share canvas size, padding and pivot with their pose.

## Terrain

The terrain has two looks, chosen by the visual style setting (`apps/web/src/visual-style.ts`).

**Original** has no sprites. Floor, Walls, Cover, the Overwatch Nest, the Prime Zone tint and the Drop Point markers are shapes computed from the grid and a view angle (`apps/web/src/terrain.ts`), with all colours in `apps/web/src/terrain-style.ts`. The tile diamond is 128 x 64 px.

**Pixel art** (the default) builds the Reactor Deck from the tileset "Notstrom": `public/assets/reactor-deck/notstrom.png` and `notstrom.json` (the atlas, PixiJS sprite sheet format) and `tileset.json` (variants, glows, wall texts, light). Its tiles are 32 x 16 px, one fixed view; the board's world is four times that, so a sprite of the tileset is scaled by 4 and anchored on `gridToWorld` of its tile (`PIXEL_SCALE` in `apps/web/src/pixel-terrain.ts`) and Heroes and VFX keep their size. Which frame goes on which tile is `pixelLayout` in that file, after `docs/design/reactor-deck/tools/assemble.js`. The floor, its decals and the light map are baked into one texture, again when the map's Cover changes; Walls, Cover, plants and the core are sprites sorted with the Heroes by depth. What the tileset has no frame for stays drawn by the game in both styles: Cover a Hero placed, destroyed Cover, the wreck of an Exhausted Hero, the ownership look of the Prime Zone, the Scout Drone area and the Fog of War.

## Heroes (canvas 128 x 192, anchor 0.5, 1; feet 8 px above the bottom edge)

Path: `heroes/<class>/<pose>`, class one of `bruiser`, `controller`, `assassin`.

| Pose | Classes | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `idle` | all | Planning, waiting |
| `move` | all | Move actions |
| `attack_ranged` | all | Most Attack cards |
| `block_shield` | all | Block 2, Shield effects |
| `utility_cast` | all | Utility cards, Overdrives |
| `hit` | all | Deck Damage feedback |
| `exhausted` | all | Exhaustion |
| `heavy_attack`, `breach_charge_throw` | bruiser | Slam Shot family, Breach Charge |
| `drone_deploy`, `cover_deploy` | controller | Scout Drone, Deploy Cover |
| `precision_aim`, `hidden_crouch` | assassin | Precision attacks and Long Shot, Hidden |

27 Hero sprites in total (7 shared x 3 classes + 6 class-specific).

### Pixel art style: sprite sheets

The Pixel art style (`?style=pixel`, the default) does not use the poses above. It plays the sprite sheets of `apps/web/public/assets/pixel-heroes/<class>/` (format and clips: `docs/design/pixel-heroes/README.md`; they are loaded by `apps/web/src/pixel-heroes.ts`, not through the manifest). The poses above stay valid for the Original style, which still draws the shape rig.

- **Frame** 136 x 104 px, feet at (58, 86), 20 frames per second, facing right and mirrored (`scale.x = -1`) for the left. Scaled by the whole number `PIXEL_SCALE` (2) with nearest-neighbour filtering, so a Hero is about 90 px tall at game size.
- **Which clip is which sheet** is `pixelSheetOf` in `pixel-heroes.ts`; a test checks that every game clip of every class has a sheet. Fallbacks: a clip a class lacks plays its basic attack (a Long Shot of a Bruiser is `slam`), and the Controller and the Assassin, which have no clip for a hit the Shield absorbs, stand in `idle` (the Shield mark of the game carries the hit). `inCover` and `inCoverFull` are the loop of `half` and `full` after the taking. Which clip plays when, and for how long, stays with `playback.ts` and `hero-clips.ts`: the sheet is played at the progress of the rig clip it stands for.
- **Layers.** On the board only the `figure` layer is drawn: the game draws the Shield bubble, the damage numbers, the Wait mark, the Cover, the Scout Drone and the shots itself (`effects-layer.ts`, `board-drawing.ts`). The Hero Select draws all three layers (muzzle flash, tracer and the Hidden label are baked in there).
- **From `clips.json`:** `ringRedFrames` flashes the Squad ring red, `ringAlpha` fades the Squad ring, `shake` offsets the sprite. The Squad colour is the ring the renderer draws; the sprites carry no team colour. The Hidden look of the Assassin is the figure fading to the translucent look it keeps.

## Manifest

`assets/manifest.json` is a PixiJS asset manifest (one bundle per folder: `heroes.<class>`, `vfx`). Each asset has an `alias` (`<bundle>.<name>`, for example `heroes.bruiser.idle`), a relative `src` and `data.anchor`. The game loads only through the manifest. When final assets are packed into atlases, the manifest changes, the aliases do not.

## Rendering notes (from the 45 x 25 Grid)

- A full Grid is 4480 px wide at 128 px tiles ((45 + 25) x 64). On a phone the board is therefore scaled down; the UI spec defines the camera behaviour.
- Depth sorting by the rotated depth of the anchor tile (`row + column` at the 45 degree view; skill `depth-sorting-occlusion`).
- Tile centre for grid cell `(col, row)`: `x = (col - row) * 64`, `y = (col + row) * 32` (skill `isometric-grid-math`).
