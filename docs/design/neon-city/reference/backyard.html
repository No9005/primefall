Nachtbrand: the backyard

One scene instead of a whole board: a walled courtyard behind the tenements. A burning car and burning barrels, a crater in the tar, dumpsters, a chain-link fence, a clothesline between the walls, steam from a manhole. Fire escapes and the roof of an annex are the high ground. Rain, flames, smoke, steam and flickering neon are live. Same 32 × 16 isometric pixel scale as the other maps.

Burning car and barrels (Full Cover candidates) Dumpsters, crates, wreck (Half Cover candidates) Chain-link fence (blocks movement, not sight) Crater and potholes (look only for now) Fire escapes and the annex roof (high ground)

The yard

The tenement on the back wall and the one on the left close the yard; the fence and the gate at the front are the way in. The annex on the right has a flat roof with a shack, reached by the fire escape on its wall.

Reading

Walls block movement and Line of Sight. The fence blocks movement but you can see through it. Dumpsters and crates are Half Cover, burning things Full Cover.

Mood

The yard is lit by the fire and by two neon signs. With reduced motion the page shows one still frame.
