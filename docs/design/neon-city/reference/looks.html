Neon City: tar, fire and fire escapes

A megacity at night. Wet, broken tar with potholes and craters, burning cars as cover, towers with lit windows and neon, and high ground on fire escapes and parking garages. Rain, flames, smoke and flickering signs are live. Same 32 × 16 isometric pixel scale as the Notstrom Map. A first look, nothing is built into the game.

One crossing to judge the look before any layout: a tenement with a fire escape, a parking garage with a burning car on its roof, a corporate tower with a setback, a shop with signs, a pothole field and wrecks in the street.

Layout

Crossfire Boulevard

The Crossroads structure on the 45 × 25 board: a wide boulevard and an avenue cross in a plaza with the Prime Zone, tenements and garages take the quarters, the Outposts sit on the avenue, the Drop Points at the ends of the boulevard. The quarters are mirrored in a twist: garages and tenements swap places so the two Squads do not start with the same high ground.

Reading

Towers are walls: they block movement and Line of Sight. Wrecks are Half Cover, burning cars and buses Full Cover. The long streets give open lines of sight, the plaza is the contested ground.

Tar

Potholes and craters are a look first. As a rule they could be difficult terrain (a step in costs more), or Half Cover when deep. Open.

High ground

Fire escapes and garage decks give a view over the streets. High ground is not in the rules (ADR 0020), so this layout shows the look only.

Mood

Rain, fire, smoke and the flicker of the signs run live. With reduced motion the page shows one still frame.

Not decided here

Whether burning cars hurt (a fire that spreads is a new rule), whether rain changes Line of Sight or sound, and how the garage decks are entered. A proposal to discuss: a deck is entered only by its ramp or its fire-escape tile, a Hero on a deck sees two tiles further and loses Hidden while the street can see it.
