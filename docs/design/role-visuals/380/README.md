# Role effects on the board: the looks approved for #380

Stills of the design canvas (page "New role visuals", boards J to N and W). The owner approved them overall on 13 November 2026. **Build it the way the images show it; where text and images disagree, the images win.** The figures in the stills are the game's own, drawn at 2 x game size, and the small tags are the labels the game puts on effects. The cover and badge looks of `../README.md` (natural and built Cover, the drop, the buff badge) are already built (#384) and stay as they are.
